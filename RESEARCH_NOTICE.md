# Research Notice

The performance evidence in this repository is simulated. It is not live
trading performance, investment advice, an offer, or a forecast of future
returns.

The public Python package is an executable architecture example built on
synthetic inputs. It implements a selected subset of the research controls:
information timing, announcement-aware exits, exposure caps, whole-pair
execution availability, funding and cost accounting, reconciliation, and
audit hashing. It does not contain the private signal models, empirical
thresholds, covariance estimator, margin engine, raw market data, complete
promotion pipeline, or the implementation that generated the reported
performance evidence.

Historical market archives are not distributed by this repository. Aggregate
figures are retained only to document the frozen research result and its
limitations.
